About the app

HereShift helps developers and authorized testers simulate a stationary virtual location on compatible Android devices. Choose a point on a map or a clearly labeled public place, preview the coordinates, and use Android-supported testing features with explicit user authorization. Start and stop your simulation clearly.

HereShift does not change your physical location, cellular network, IP address or legal eligibility, and other apps may identify or reject simulated locations. No real-location recording or friend-sharing is required for the proposed initial version. Feature availability depends on device and Android version.
